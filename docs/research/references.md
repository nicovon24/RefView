### RefView inferences

- A short authored `Moment` is a suitable early unit because it concentrates production effort on readable football action; this is a RefView decision, not copied content.
- RefView should prioritize spatial evidence, referee positioning, and debrief over licensed spectacle, career simulation, or a large feature count.
- Natural proportions, movement, ball contact, lighting, sound, and comfortable cameras remain ambitious quality targets to validate in a running browser scene. Official marketing descriptions do not prove those qualities.

RefView must not copy any reference's branding, layout, footage, stadium, kits, characters, or assets. A durable RefView visual world remains to be selected with Nicolas before `DESIGN.md` is created.
