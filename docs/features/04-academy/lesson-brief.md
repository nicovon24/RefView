## Identity and purpose

- Lesson ID: `careless-trip-in-penalty-area`
- Proposed version: 1
- Edition: IFAB Laws of the Game 2026/27
- Topic: Law 12 challenge and Law 14 penalty restart
- Learner outcome: distinguish what must be observed from the rule applied to an established careless trip in the defender's own penalty area.

## Learning sequence

1. **Plain explanation:** A careless trip with contact while the ball is in play is a direct-free-kick offence. If committed inside the offender's own penalty area, the restart is a penalty kick. Carelessness alone does not require a card; separate sanction grounds must still be checked.
2. **Authored example facts:** The attacker is onside; the ball remains in play; a defender trips the attacker inside the defender's area; the contact is careless; a covering defender prevents an obvious-goal-scoring-opportunity interpretation; there is no separate misconduct. These are RefView's authored facts, not claims established by the IFAB text or current animation.
3. **Evidence to inspect:** the actual foot/leg contact, where the offence occurs relative to the penalty-area boundary, the ball's state, and the covering defender. A poor referee position can obscure contact even though authored facts stay fixed.
4. **Interpretation:** decide whether observed contact is a trip, whether it is careless or more serious, whether it is inside/on the line, and whether a separate caution or sending-off ground exists. Do not infer these from a pose label alone.
5. **Rule application:** on these authored facts, stop play, award a penalty kick, and show no card.

## Prepared follow-up candidates

- Why a penalty rather than a direct free kick at the spot? The Law 14 restart applies because the direct-free-kick offence is inside the offender's own area.
- Why no yellow card? Careless conduct alone needs no disciplinary sanction; this example has no separate cautionable fact.
- What if the contact is on the penalty-area line? Law 1 treats the boundary line as part of the area.
- What if the challenge is reckless? Law 12 requires a caution, subject to the complete circumstances.
