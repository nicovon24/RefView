# Full-product interactive concept

Status is tracked only in [`../../specs/tasks.md`](../../specs/tasks.md).

**Delegated SDD.** This feature selects a durable RefView visual direction and makes the principal product journeys navigable before their production backends exist.

## Objective

Create an original, responsive, bilingual interactive concept covering Simulator, Academy, Community, Assessments, Profile, and Coach while labelling every illustrated or fixture-backed state honestly.

## Observable acceptance

- **CON-REQ-001:** A reviewed `DESIGN.md` records the visual world, tokens, typography, spacing, motion, accessibility, and simulator-specific HUD principles without copying a reference product.
- **CON-REQ-002:** Spanish is the initial locale and the complete concept can switch to English.
- **CON-REQ-003:** Principal journeys and meaningful loading, empty, error, locked, fixture, and completed states are navigable.
- **CON-REQ-004:** The active simulator pitch remains visually dominant; VAR prioritizes synchronized evidence; debrief prioritizes facts, interpretation, references, and next practice.
- **CON-REQ-005:** Responsive application surfaces work at desktop and mobile widths; desktop-only simulator controls are identified rather than faked on mobile.
- **CON-REQ-006:** Illustrative content, fictional matches, prepared Coach responses, and unimplemented actions are visibly labelled.
- **CON-REQ-007:** Directly inspected references and design inferences remain distinguishable in the reference register.

## Limits

- This concept is not evidence of 3D animation quality, database behavior, AI capability, moderation, or production readiness.
- Do not copy Premier League Player, another simulator, a real competition, club, stadium, broadcast package, or character.
- Do not add payments or imply willingness to pay.

## Evidence

- Impeccable visual-direction decision and durable design artifacts.
- Playwright CLI journey and responsive checks.
- `web-design-guidelines` accessibility/design audit.
- Desktop/mobile screenshots and independent review.
