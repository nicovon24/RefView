## Asset inspection and visual result

- Base body: 14,318 triangles, 69 nodes, three meshes/materials, seven image references, one 65-joint skin, approximately 1.81 m high. Hair: 1,301 triangles and two image references. Each actor clones the skeleton and runs its own animation mixer; geometry and source textures are reused.
- The library contains 43 clips. The audition exposes `Idle_Loop`, `Walk_Loop`, `Jog_Fwd_Loop` and `Sprint_Loop`. It has generic push and roll/fall-like clips, but no authored football tackle, contact, stop/turn or referee-specific clip. Those actions need a separate matching source or authored retargeting.
- [Match camera capture](../../../artifacts/player-audition-number3.png) and [close capture](../../../artifacts/player-audition-close.png) show improved continuous limb deformation, short dark hair and identifiable colored teams. The test jersey, shorts, socks, boots and numbers are shader/decal trials, not a finished kit. The source body is visibly superhero-proportioned, especially close up; this candidate should not be promoted unchanged as the final football player.
- Locomotion currently plays in place. Root displacement is intentionally absent, so foot planting and phase/velocity sync with the canonical track remain unproven. A screenshot of a single frame cannot establish animation quality over the contact/fall window. The present canvas is a visual laboratory, not a calibrated replacement.

## Athletic silhouette comparison

The default `Athletic` / `Deportiva` variant narrows the upper-body mesh by up to 10% laterally and 18% in depth over a tapered torso/shoulder band, recomputes normals and removes the exaggerated body normal map. `Original` restores the source proportions. The change preserves vertex count, skin weights, bone names, animations and source textures. It is a reversible runtime comparison, not a new asset or an approved final footballer. [Athletic close view](../../../artifacts/player-athletic-close.png) and [source close view](../../../artifacts/player-source-close.png) were inspected at the same camera and motion setting. The athletic silhouette reads less muscular, but its shoulders and sleeves still need a purpose-built football kit or a better base mesh.

The free Standard archive was inspected and contains only Superhero Male/Female bodies; Quaternius lists Regular proportions in the full pack but the [Source download requires payment](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters), which was not made. Two other free CC0 candidates were reviewed from their creators' descriptions: [Orange Juice Games' male base mesh](https://orange-juice-games.itch.io/male-base-mesh) is a generic 43 kB rigged archive with no demonstrated football kit or compatible skeleton, and [Plewr's low-poly character](https://plewr.itch.io/3d-rigged-character) likewise has no demonstrated compatibility with these Quaternius clips. Retargeting and visual quality would need direct asset inspection. Neither was substituted on description alone.

## Regular-proportion mesh research, not a selected player

A further focused `gpt-6-luna`/`high` read-only inspection (runtime identity unconfirmed) retrieved [Orange Juice Games' CC0 male base mesh from its creator page](https://orange-juice-games.itch.io/male-base-mesh) without an account or payment. The source ZIP is 44,526 bytes, SHA-256 `835D0E1D65EE0615BD41CD20B449337AF6882265BE9A3B7BD4086E3EA49E22ED`; its `male_base_mesh.glb` is 93,936 bytes, SHA-256 `206CB651484BAF9AAA29A030A38FEECBE29575C3E1CBD820A58FA343C2AE14C4`. Both are kept only in `artifacts/rig-research/`, not copied to the public app or canonical content. The GLB contains one 1,226-vertex / 1,804-triangle mesh primitive, 56 skin joints, UVs, and no embedded material, texture or animation. Its adult male silhouette is more regular than the superhero candidate, but it has no face, hair, kit, boots or football motion. The bone names follow Blender conventions rather than the documented Mixamo/Quaternius mapping; `spine.005` parenting and the absent explicit skeleton root need direct import/deformation checks. Compatibility with the Universal Animation Library is unproven. [MakeHuman/MPFB rig documentation](https://www.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/characters/rig.html) remains the more explicit Mixamo-rig path, though its generated output also needs audition. This research does not satisfy RFP-REQ-001 or authorize replacing the seven incident actors.

The isolated viewer now offers three reusable kit treatments (classic, vertical stripes and sash), plus front, close and match cameras. Dark brown eyebrows, reduced eye specularity, a fixed matte body response and restrained red facial-color correction make the face less glossy and more natural. Bind-space fragment tests replace the old interpolated numeric kit regions, eliminating false intermediate shirt/shorts/sock bands. Shirt, shorts and boot shells clone the body's skin indices/weights, bind to the actor's independent skeleton and sit slightly above the body surface; the outlines of the garment/boot pieces have more depth than painted color alone. The jersey numbers are sampled in bind-space onto the skinned torso and shirt material, with a dark outline for contrast. Unlike the earlier rigid back decal, they stay on the torso during sprint animation. These remain procedural trial garments, without a final textile mesh or footwear construction.
