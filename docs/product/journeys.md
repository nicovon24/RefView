# RefView product journeys

These journeys define the full product horizon. Only the first simulator incident is currently specified for implementation.

## Simulator

1. Choose role, then a scenario or compatible session.
2. Read a short briefing with role, match context, controls, and competition settings, without hidden answers.
3. Enter the active pitch with minimal overlays.
4. Move, look, observe, and communicate within the role's task.
5. Stop or continue, then confirm restart and discipline where applicable.
6. Receive a deterministic debrief separating decision, observation/positioning, communication, and protocol.
7. Replay the same canonical timeline using only perspectives now permitted.
8. Retry or continue to the next authored sequence.

Desktop keyboard/mouse is the first simulator target. The surrounding product is responsive; mobile simulator controls are later work.

## Academy

1. Explore by Law or football situation: offside, handball, advantage, challenges, penalties, discipline, restarts, or VAR.
2. Start with a plain-language explanation.
3. Inspect an authored example and the observable facts.
4. Open a more technical explanation and the precise versioned official reference.
5. Ask the Coach a grounded follow-up or use prepared responses.
6. Launch a related simulator case or assessment.

## Community

1. Explore distinct match records identified by teams, date, and competition.
2. Open a fictional demonstration match page in the prototype.
3. Inspect rating count and distribution, then submit or revise one's own rating.
4. Read comments that distinguish opinion, evidence, and reviewed education.
5. Write a comment prompted to explain the decision and available evidence.
6. Like or dislike comments without treating popularity as the Laws answer.

No prototype claim may invent a real referee performance or rating.

## Assessments

1. Choose a topic, level, or recommended practice.
2. Answer rule questions, select restarts/sanctions, or analyze authored incidents.
3. Add brief reasoning when the exercise requires it.
4. Receive a reasoned, versioned correction.
5. See which facts, rule application, or interpretation need practice.
6. Continue into a targeted lesson or simulator case.

## Profile and preferences

- Learning progress by topic and rules edition.
- Simulator attempts and separate evaluation dimensions.
- Saved lessons, cases, and community content.
- Language, accessibility, input, camera, motion, and quality preferences.
- Anonymous first-MVP attempts may later attach to an account through an explicit migration flow.

## AI Coach

The Coach appears after an attempt, within Academy explanations, and after assessments. It answers from permitted attempt evidence, curated facts, deterministic results, and versioned references. Challenge mode never exposes hidden answers. When the provider is unavailable, prepared-response mode is labelled and the rest of RefView still works.
