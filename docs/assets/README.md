# Asset and licence register

The static prototype uses two generated scene images and two open-licensed fonts. Their exact origins, notices, and prototype use are recorded in [`../prototype/assets/README.md`](../prototype/assets/README.md). No external animation, audio, stadium, player model, or texture asset has been selected for the playable scene.

Before an asset enters the repository, record:

- Asset name and exact source URL.
- Author/publisher and licence version.
- Download/acquisition date and original file name.
- Allowed uses, attribution, redistribution, and modification conditions.
- Where the asset is used and any modifications made.
- Whether it is temporary, prototype-only, or production-eligible.

Do not purchase an asset until the working scene identifies a specific gap that free or original material cannot adequately validate.
